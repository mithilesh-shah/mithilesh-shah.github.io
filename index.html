Studying markets where climate, technology, and everyday economic life meet.

I am Mithilesh Shah, a PhD student in Marketing at London Business School. My work studies how people, firms, and workers make decisions in emerging markets when infrastructure, climate shocks, and AI change the constraints around them.

research

Questions I Keep Returning To

01

How do households update aspirations?

I study how exposure to new places, opportunities, and information can change investments in human capital, especially education.

02

What makes climate action adoptable?

My work examines how marketing frames and local delivery shape environmentally beneficial consumer behavior.

03

Can AI improve economic outcomes?

I am interested in whether AI-enabled training and AI agents can help workers and small firms improve income, productivity, and business outcomes.

Research in Circulation

Revise & Resubmit at Marketing Science

Highways and Growth in Consumer Demand in Education: Insights from India

With Thomas Zhang, Rajesh Chandy, Om Narasimhan

Using India's Golden Quadrilateral highway construction as a natural experiment, this paper shows that rural households newly able to visit urban areas increased their spending on education.

Revise & Resubmit, Registered Report at Journal of Consumer Research

Marketing Trees: Evidence from a Field Experiment in India

With Catherine Cole, Gita Johar, Rajesh Chandy, Gaurav Mehta and SCP collaborators

A large-scale field experiment in rural India tests motivational frames encouraging households to buy and plant trees.
