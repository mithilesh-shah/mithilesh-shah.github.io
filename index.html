doctoral student in marketing

Mithilesh Pradeep Shobha Shah

I am a doctoral student in Marketing at London Business School studying how markets evolve when people, firms, and technologies meet under conditions of constraint.

My research is grounded in emerging markets, where climate shocks, infrastructure, entrepreneurship, and AI are reshaping everyday economic decisions. I ask how these forces influence what households invest in, how firms grow, and how workers adapt.

Methodologically, I combine field experiments, quasi-experimental designs, survey research, and primary data collection. Substantively, I am interested in marketing that helps explain development challenges and design better interventions for a changing world.

Revise & Resubmit at Marketing Science

Highways and Growth in Consumer Demand in Education: Insights from India

With Thomas Zhang, Rajesh Chandy, Om Narasimhan

Using India's Golden Quadrilateral highway construction as a natural experiment, this paper shows that rural households newly able to visit urban areas increased spending on education.

Revise & Resubmit, Registered Report at Journal of Consumer Research

Marketing Trees: Evidence from a Field Experiment in India

With Catherine Cole, Gita Johar, Rajesh Chandy, Gaurav Mehta and SCP collaborators

A large-scale field experiment in rural India tests motivational frames encouraging households to buy and plant trees.
